ABOUT

A simple idea
for a better setup.

MyCam lets you use your phone as a high quality webcam for your PC. It's free, open source, and built for everyone who needs a better camera without the extra cost.

Better camera

Use your phone's high quality camera.

Simple & fast

Get started in minutes with no complex setup.

Privacy first

Works on your local network. No ads. No tracking.

Open source

Built by the community, for the community.

Built in the open

MyCam is open source. You can view the code, report issues, contribute, or suggest new features on GitHub.
