HOW IT WORKS

Get started in
three simple steps.

Turn your phone into a webcam in just a few minutes. No complicated setup. No extra hardware.

1

Install MyCam

Download and install MyCam on your Windows PC. Install the Android app, or use your iPhone's own browser — nothing to install there.

Download for Windows Get Android APK
2

Connect your phone

Android: plug in over USB, or pair once over Wi-Fi with a 6-digit code. iPhone: scan a QR code shown by MyCam right in Safari.

3

Use as webcam

Select MyCam in your favourite app (Zoom, Discord, OBS, Teams, and more) and start streaming your phone's camera.
