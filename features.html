FEATURES

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

MyCam gives you a simple and reliable way to use your phone as a webcam, with all the essential features and no unnecessary complexity.

USB or Wi-Fi

Connect your way. Fast, stable and easy to use.

High quality video

Up to 4K at 120 FPS (depending on your device).

Front & back camera

Use any camera on your phone.

Full controls

Zoom, focus, torch and more right from your PC.

Privacy first

Your data stays on your local network. No ads. No tracking.

Works with your apps

Use MyCam in Zoom, Discord, OBS, Google Meet and more.
